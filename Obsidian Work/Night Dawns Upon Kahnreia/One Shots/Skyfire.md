_Skyfire_ is a pre-campaign one-shot featuring [[Lilith Serafall]] before the events of _[[Night Dawns Upon Kahnreia]]_. It is not required to play the main campaign and may be experienced either before or after meeting Lilith in the primary story. When played beforehand, it serves as the party’s introduction to Lilith. When played afterward, it functions as a flashback-style side story that reveals one of the events behind her reputation.


As Skyfire is not required to play the main campaign, some players may experience it before meeting Lilith in the main story, while others play it afterward as a flashback-style side story that reveals one of the events behind her reputation.

The one-shot begins at a noble event where the party has been hired as guards.

When dragon-riders attack, several nobles are abducted. The party spends the rest of the one-shot fighting through assassins and searching for a way to catch up to the dragon-riders.

During the adventure, the party encounters Lilith Serafall.

She appears as a white-haired, red-eyed noblewoman in a fabulous dress whose skirt she has torn for mobility. When the party finds her, she is beating the snot out of a half-orc assassin or bandit.

Lilith helps the party catch up to the dragon-riders.

The climax of Skyfire involves combat on the taut wings of dragons, hundreds of feet in the air.
