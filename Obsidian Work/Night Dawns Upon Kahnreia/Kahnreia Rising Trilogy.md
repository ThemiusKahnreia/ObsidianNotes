Kahnreia Rising is the story made from the Prequel campaign: [[Age Of Moonlight]] detailing the old heroic age and the founding of the great nation of [[Kahnreia]], the main story arc: [[Night Dawns Upon Kahnreia]], as [[Themius V Kahnreia]] retakes his homeland and fights off a threat he is compelled to by [[Astaroth]], and finally, the end of Themius' story: [[Astaroth Unleashed]] and its traversal of [[The Abyss]] culminating in Astaroth's freedom.
