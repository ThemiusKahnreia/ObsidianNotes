The world of Korada is a large world defined by a mass ocean surrounding its few large continents and many archipelagoes.

The major continents are [[Klasore]] "the main continent", [[Laos]] the island nation, [[Dratlan]] the dragon continent, and [[Mahdleika]] the lost continent.

It is explored through the various one-shot adventures and larger campaigns, the most prevalent of which are the [[Kahnreia Rising Trilogy]].

It is also home to many one-shots, stories that take place quickly. Quite a number of these small stories tell you small tidbits of a certain NPC's character, or their past (or future) struggles.
