The Long Preparation was the time period in which Themius travelled the world after his return to "life" via the help of Astaroth.

He would study many concepts, forms of conduct, politics, magics, swordsmanship, and so much more. Especially so, since this age in Themius' Journey took almost 500 years.

During this time, he studies under many people:
- [[Lady Ira Fikvolor]] - The last true [[Mage Hunter]].
- [[Master Viron Darak]] - A high-ranking member of the [[Darakes]].
- [[Taih'lya Dzantia]] - The previous Queen of [[Dzantia]], and mother to [[Dara Dzantia]].
- [[Karth Salema]] - The Martyr, chosen servant of the [[Celestial of Light]].
- [[Ischo Akal]] - The [[Akashase]] of the [[Akashan Warrior Clan]].
These name some of the most influential.

He also meets many new people who look up to him, but many of which also consider him something much more important... friend.
- [[Lilith Serafall]], a Mist Vampire monk.
- [[Ark Lightning]], the Celestial of Lightning
- [[Jeih'lyo Lazatham]], a street kid that Themius mentors into becoming a respectable scholar of the arcane (and a pyromancer of high regard).
- [[Zahren Zayrotras]], a magical engineer of extreme intelligence.
- The previously mentioned [[Karth Salema]] and [[Dara Dzantia]].
- [[Victoir Rashenfrost]], a half-living master swordsman
- [[Velsarai]], an elder dragon.
These people, save maybe for Dara Dzantia, form Themius' force known initially as Vaelen. Vaelen is a word from ancient common that meant "outcast".
