The specific part of the brain (slightly different brain structure on Korada than on Earth) that conceptualizes, structuralizes, and executes magic formula for a spellcaster whenever they need to sustain or cast magic.

If too much magic is processed, whether through sustaining a spell beyond the means to do so, or casting a spell too complex for your engram to handle, you can enter [[Engram Overload]], which has a 88.6% lethality rate, due to it causing your engram to run rampant, and can discharge all of your remaining mana like a wild magic surge, but most commonly like a localized nuclear bomb. 

There are many tiers of effect to [[Engram Overload]], so there are cases where it is less lethal, but should not be pushed further in case of... well... "kaboom".

In my setting, this is why spellcasters get more magic as they level up. Sure, they train their mana reserves as well (generally), but they also are training the most common limiting factor to magic: their Engram.
