The tale of how [[Themius V Kahnreia]] ventured into [[The Abyss]], though cast there by his now-mortal-enemy, [[Michael, Lord of Angels]]. This campaign begins with the [[The Abyss Expedition]], thwarted in thought by Michael, forcing Themius and party to descend the layers, as fighting upward into Hell's territory (and likely Asmodeus' territory directly) is near-suicide, due to the sharp increase in danger after breaking free of the Abyss.
