The Celestials are the major gods. They control the elements, and influence the balance of the world, reigning from each of their elemental planes.

However, they can choose to go to the material plane (where they become mortal, while remaining quite powerful), though this causes them great danger, so most stay out of such business, unlike the old days of the [[Moonlit Age]], when celestials lived partially on the material plane to rule and govern the laws binding nature.


There is a celestial for each element.

- The [[Celestial of Shadow]] - Aralas Shade
- The [[Celestial of Light]] - [[Anastasia Dawn]]
- The [[Celestial of Nature]] (Also "Celestial of Life") - Inah Flores
- The [[Celestial of Stone]] - Jinlan Fahl
- The [[Celestial of Frost]] - [[Akasha Rime]]
- The [[Celestial of Lightning]] - [[Ark Lightning]]
- The [[Celestial of Ember]] - Sivas Kaskenof
- The [[Celestial of Sky]] - Naroma Gail
- The [[Celestial of Ocean]] - Tyros Maun
