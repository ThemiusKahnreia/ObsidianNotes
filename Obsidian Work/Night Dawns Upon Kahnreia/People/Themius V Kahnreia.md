5171 H.A.
Birth of Themius Vartelian Sylvanor Aurelias Kahnreia.

Born in [[Kaveran]], capital of [[Kahnreia]], as the third son of King [[Tarsis V Kahnreia]]. As a member of the Old Kahnreian royal line, he possesses the dormant [[Kahnreian Mystic Eyes]], which are trained to awakening by the age of 15.

The Kahnreian royal family traditionally practiced a meritocratic succession system. While the throne almost always passed through the eldest son, this was due to rigorous preparation and expectations placed upon the heir rather than strict primogeniture. Royal men traditionally took three wives and produced six children, helping ensure the continuation of the royal line and preserving alliances with other peoples.

Through generations of intermarriage, Themius possesses distant aasimar and tiefling ancestry. While this grants him an extended lifespan and exceptional vitality, it provides little innate magical talent due to the lineages' magic cancelling out.

5209 H.A.
Promotion to rank of captain at the age of thirty-eight.

Receives command responsibilities and begins service as the captain of the 65th special division in the Kahnreian military.

5221 H.A.
Death of Themius.

Dies while leading troops in battle at fifty years of age.

Following his death, his soul is intercepted by Astaroth and taken to the [[Blackened Cathedral]], also known as the Cathedral of Nightmares, located within the Eleventh Layer of the Abyss.

[[Astaroth]], sealed beyond the reach of conventional time and unable to freely influence other planes, chooses Themius for reasons not yet fully understood.

5378 H.A.
Resurrection.

After one hundred fifty-seven years of death, Themius is returned to life by Astaroth.

He returns as a revenant rather than a true living human.

Through [[Astaroth]], he gains access to an immense external source of mana, allowing him to pursue magical disciplines far beyond what would otherwise have been possible.

5378–5868 H.A.
[[The Long Preparation]].

For nearly five centuries, Themius travels throughout the known world of Korada.

He studies governments, cultures, military doctrines, magical traditions, economics, diplomacy, religion, and history.

His objective is singular:

To become the best king he can possibly be before attempting to reclaim [[Kahnreia]].

During this period he develops mastery of [[Spatial Magic]] and establishes a reputation under numerous identities, including the adventuring alias [[Reyan]].

5863 H.A.
First Meeting with [[Lilith Serafall]].

While operating as an adventurer and mercenary, Themius accepts a contract involving a Boneclaw.

During the mission he encounters the [[Mist Vampire]], Lilith Serafall, whose companions have abandoned her in fear while she continues fighting the creature alone.

Recognizing both her courage and skill, he joins the battle.

The two successfully slay the Boneclaw together and begin a friendly relationship that will ultimately shape the rest of their lives.

5866 H.A.
Extreme Engram Overload.

While serving as court mage to [[Dara Dzantia]]—a position used as cover while preparing for the [[Liberation of Kahnreia]]—a terrorist attack destroys the foundations of [[Castle Dzantia]].

With almost no warning, Themius casts a massive gravity spell - working across the entire structure in an attempt to prevent its collapse.

The spell succeeds.

For nearly five minutes he supports the weight of an entire castle through raw magical force.

The effort catastrophically overloads his [[Engram]], the portion of the brain responsible for magical formula processing.

Themius enters a catatonic state for approximately one month and suffers lasting neurological damage.

Recovery continues throughout the events of [[Night Dawns Upon Kahnreia]].

5868 H.A.
[[Night Dawns Upon Kahnreia]].

The culmination of nearly five centuries of preparation.

Themius begins the campaign to reclaim Kahnreia and restore the rightful royal line.

Despite ongoing recovery from engram damage, he emerges as the central figure of the liberation effort.
