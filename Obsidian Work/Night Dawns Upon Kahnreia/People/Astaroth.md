II. PHYSICAL DESCRIPTION

* True Form:
  Unknown.

* Common Manifestation:
  A tall figure clad in dark vestments and armor, often associated with darkness, stars, voids, and spatial distortions.

* Distinguishing Features:

  * Spatial distortions frequently accompany his presence.
  * Appears exhausted despite his immense age.
  * Carries himself more like a ruler burdened by responsibility than a conqueror.

III. PERSONALITY

* Core Traits:

  * Secretive
  * Stoic

* Virtues:

  * Keeps his word.
  * Genuinely values creation.
  * Will sacrifice himself to protect others.
  * Possesses immense patience.

* Flaws:

  * Rarely explains his reasoning.
  * Habitually manipulates events from the shadows.

  * Preserve reality.
  * Protect creation.
  * Prevent Geradul's return.
  * Ensure the world can survive after his eventual death.

IV. HISTORY

The First Age - [[The Age of Making]]

At the dawn of creation, Astaroth existed as [[Azathoth]], the God of Reality.

Azathoth was responsible for establishing many of the fundamental structures that govern existence itself.

Believing reality required guardians beyond himself, Azathoth created [[The Celestials]].

The Celestials were entrusted with governing elemental and conceptual forces while helping sustain the Weave.

Together they established what would become known as [[The Rule of Gods]].

The Emergence of Geradul

At some point during the First Age, Azathoth's antithesis revealed itself.

This being was [[Geradul]].

Where Azathoth represented reality and creation, Geradul represented corruption, contradiction, and unmaking.

Geradul unleashed a curse upon existence.

The curse defied the fundamental laws of reality.

The First War

The Celestials fought alongside Azathoth against Geradul.

The original [[Celestial of Shadow]] led much of the resistance.

During the conflict, Azathoth realized that destroying Geradul would condemn the Celestial of Shadow to a cursed death.

Unwilling to sacrifice one of his creations, Azathoth chose to bear the burden himself.

He struck down Geradul.

[[The Fall of Azathoth]]

Azathoth survived.

The curse did not allow him to remain unchanged.

The power of Time was torn from his being.

The remainder of his divine essence became corrupted.

The other gods believed Azathoth had perished.

His surviving spirit reformed under a new identity.

That identity became Astaroth.

[[The Birth of the Time Gods]]

The loss of Azathoth's temporal authority created a vacuum within reality.

Three new Gods of Time emerged to divide and govern the separated power.

Meanwhile, Geradul's corpse became the foundation of the Abyss itself.

The Abyss was subsequently sealed beneath Hell.

The Exile

The surviving gods never realized that Astaroth was Azathoth.

They saw only a corrupted divine being of immense power.

Believing him dangerous, they imprisoned him.

Astaroth was sealed within the Eleventh Layer of the Abyss.

The Eleventh Layer was separated from the Tenth Layer and isolated by the newly formed Gods of Time.

Though fragments of his influence could escape, Astaroth himself remained trapped.

[[The Long Imprisonment]]

For millennia Astaroth watched creation from confinement.

Civilizations rose and fell.

Kingdoms flourished and vanished.

The world slowly forgot [[The Age of Making]].

As reality weakened and ancient seals deteriorated, Astaroth found limited opportunities to intervene.

Many historical events, campaigns, and legends bear traces of his influence.

V. THEMIUS KAHNREIA

At some point following the death of [[Themius V Kahnreia]], Astaroth intercepted the prince's soul.

The two engaged in extensive debate regarding a proposed pact.

Unlike many warlock patrons, Astaroth did not demand immediate obedience.

The exact terms of the pact remain known only to Astaroth and Themius.

Over time, several terms become apparent:

* If Themius' will breaks, his soul becomes Astaroth's servant permanently.
* If Themius fulfills an unknown objective, his soul will be protected for the remainder of Astaroth's existence.
* Their souls are linked.
* Themius became Astaroth's first and only warlock, though Astaroth has helped and aided other before, never has he formed a bond or pact with them.

Despite widespread suspicion regarding Astaroth's nature, Themius does not regard him as evil.

Themius instead views him as a deeply flawed but ultimately well-intentioned being.

Most people never realize Themius is a warlock.

His extensive magical research and mastery of spellcraft lead many to mistake him for an unusually talented wizard.

IX. NOTES FOR WRITERS AND GMS

* Astaroth is not a villain.
* Astaroth is not a hero.
* He should rarely explain himself directly.
* Most information regarding his motives should be inferred through actions rather than exposition.
* Players should frequently suspect him of causing events he is actually attempting to prevent.
* His interventions often appear manipulative because he cannot act openly.
* The relationship between Astaroth and Themius is not intended to be a patron-abuse narrative.
* Breaking the pact is not the ultimate goal of their relationship.
* Astaroth genuinely cares for creation, but almost never admits this openly.
* Very few characters know that Astaroth was once Azathoth.
* Astaroth's selection of Themius is one of the foundational mysteries of the trilogy and is intended to be understood gradually through observation rather than direct explanation.
