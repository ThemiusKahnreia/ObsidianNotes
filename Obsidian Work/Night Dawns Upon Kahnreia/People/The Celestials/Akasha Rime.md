Akasha Rime founded the [[Akashan Warrior Clan]], a group of warriors who wield unorthodox curved blades (this world's katana-style weapons), that are, with time in service, enchanted through battle ritual, to sharpen themselves over time with the blood of enemies cut by the blade. (It does not work with the sword wielder's blood (which is used in the ritual at the start), and cannot be performed by anyone who is not the sworn wielder).

Later in her life, she became the Guardian Diety of this warrior clan, known as "The Lady of Ice", when she ascended to the position of being the [[Celestial of Frost]].

Her name was lost to time and fading memory, so she named herself after the clan, and the common finding in her original homeland: Rimefrost.
